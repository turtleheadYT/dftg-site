The game

The premise

You play as a small goose lost inside a vast, abandoned cave system. At first, the caves seem empty: just wet stone, fog, narrow paths and distant echoes. But something is watching from the darkness.

Explore dangerous tunnels, solve your way through forgotten areas, and find a path back to safety before the creature finds you.

The creature

Deep in the cave lives a towering mechanical creature with long segmented limbs, bony hands and a pale mask-like face. It hides in the darkness, clings to the cave walls, and waits for anyone who wanders too far.

You may not see it right away, but you will hear it.

Gameplay

  • Explore dark cave environments

  • Avoid a giant lurking monster

  • Find new paths and hidden areas

  • Survive with limited visibility

  • Discover the mystery beneath the caves

About the creator

This game is being created by an independent developer with a love for creepy atmosphere, strange creatures, and horror games that build tension instead of relying only on jumpscares.

The goal is to turn a simple goose into an unlikely horror-game hero: small, vulnerable, and trying to survive something far larger than itself.

Development

The game is currently in development. New environments, mechanics, creature behavior and story details may change as the project grows.
