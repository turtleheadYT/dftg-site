Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Overview

Don’t Feed The Geese is developed by Probably Turtle (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Don’t Feed The Geese and its online multiplayer features.

We access limited information only to provide reliable online multiplayer. We do not sell personal information and do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

2. Information Used for Multiplayer

Don’t Feed The Geese uses Epic Online Services (“EOS”) and Epic Account Services for player sign-in and multiplayer.

When you sign in and use online multiplayer, the game may access or use:

  • Your Epic Account identifier
  • Your Epic display name
  • Authentication and session identifiers
  • Multiplayer-session information, such as games you host, find, or join
  • Technical connection information needed to establish and maintain an online match

The game has Basic Profile access only. It does not request access to your Epic friends list or online-presence information.

The game never receives your Epic Games password or payment information.

3. How We Use Information

We use this limited information to:

  • Sign you in through your Epic Games account
  • Create, advertise, find, join, and maintain online matches
  • Display player names during multiplayer gameplay
  • Help keep online multiplayer reliable and secure

4. Player-Hosted Matches

Don’t Feed The Geese uses player-hosted multiplayer matches. One player hosts the match from their computer, and EOS helps players discover and connect to that host.

EOS may use direct peer-to-peer connectivity when available, or relay connectivity when needed. We do not operate permanent dedicated game servers for normal matches.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information is shared only when needed to operate multiplayer features. This may include:

  • Epic Games and EOS, to provide Epic sign-in and online multiplayer services
  • The host player and other connected players, where limited connection and session information must be exchanged for the match to function
  • Authorities or other parties if required by applicable law

For information about how Epic Games handles Epic Account data, please review the Epic Games Privacy Policy .

6. Storage and Retention

We do not operate a backend intended to permanently store player profiles or multiplayer-session records.

Game settings and save data may be stored locally on your device. Information used for an active online session is used only as needed to operate that session.

Epic Games may handle information associated with your Epic Account under its own privacy policy and account settings.

7. This Website

The Don’t Feed The Geese website is a static informational website. It does not require you to create an account, does not use advertising, and does not intentionally collect personal information through forms or tracking cookies.

8. Your Choices

You can manage your Epic Account and its privacy choices through Epic Games.

You can stop using the game at any time by uninstalling it. You may also contact us with questions about information handled directly by Probably Turtle.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game changes. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions about Don’t Feed The Geese, contact Probably Turtle at: ProbablyTurtleContact@gmail.com
